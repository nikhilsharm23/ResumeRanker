# JD2 — Growth For Impact UI/UX Internship

Source: [Detailed job description](https://tally.so/r/0QovpQ), text supplied by the project owner.

## Role overview

Design for Growth For Impact's sustainability platform, website and job board. This is a remote internship with a stated stipend of ₹10,000–₹15,000 per month. The team is distributed worldwide.

## Responsibilities

- Design user interfaces and responsive experiences across devices.
- Research user psychology and behaviour and use findings to guide design.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams and align solutions with business objectives.
- Create high-fidelity prototypes and design specifications in Figma.
- Join design critiques and feedback sessions; maintain consistent design language and visual identity.
- Keep up with UX/UI trends and best practices.
- Contribute to a website revamp and improve the job board using user feedback and testing during the first 90 days.

## Candidate requirements

- Open to candidates with 0–1 year of UX/UI design experience, including recent graduates or career changers.
- UI/UX design principles and human-centred design methods.
- Figma and prototyping tools.
- Strong understanding of user psychology and behaviour.
- Creativity, experimentation, attention to detail and strong aesthetic sense.
- The JD does not state a required degree or portfolio; neither is scored as a gate.
