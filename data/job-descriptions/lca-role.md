# JD1 — LCA Consultant (Chennai based / relocation)

Source: [Detailed job description](https://tally.so/r/9q5plp), text supplied by the project owner.

## Role and working conditions

- Onsite at the client location in Chennai; relocation is required, with relocation help available.
- Full-time office attendance, work hours and attendance tracking apply.
- Minimum one-year commitment and willingness to travel extensively across India.
- The client's sectors include manufacturing, construction, healthcare and corporate sustainability.

## Responsibilities

- Collect and compile Life Cycle Inventory (LCI) datasets.
- Validate and standardize LCI data for accuracy, completeness and consistency; document missing data.
- Build LCI models and conduct Life Cycle Assessment studies.
- Document modelling assumptions so datasets and models are traceable and audit-ready.
- Gather plant/assembly, resource, energy and inbound/outbound logistics data with cross-functional teams.
- Research public benchmarks in databases, reports, research papers, academic studies, regulatory filings and prior LCA research.
- Meet deadlines, deliver high-quality work, and manage client/stakeholder relationships.

## Candidate requirements

- **Experience conflict:** this detailed JD says **2–3 years** running LCA projects independently. The assignment's problem summary says **4–6 years**. The app scores against the detailed JD's 2–3 years and flags the conflict for human review.
- Hands-on experience with either GaBi or SimaPro.
- At least three completed LCA projects.
- Follow ISO 14040 and ISO 14044.
- Bachelor's or Master's degree; environmental domain is highly desired.
- Fluent spoken and written English.
- Preferred: prior LCA/EPD experience, basic business finance/accounting, and proactive learning.
